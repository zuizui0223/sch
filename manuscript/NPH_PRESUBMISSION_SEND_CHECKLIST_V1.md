# New Phytologist Viewpoint — presubmission send checklist

This checklist converts the completed Viewpoint package into the exact presubmission-enquiry handoff required by the current New Phytologist author guidance.

## Enquiry payload

Send to the Managing Editor using the current journal contact route:

1. the manuscript abstract;
2. a covering note answering the three editor questions;
3. author/correspondence details in the email signature or covering note.

Canonical source:

```text
manuscript/NPH_PRESUBMISSION_ENQUIRY_V1.md
```

The existing enquiry already contains the abstract and all three required questions, with each answer held under the journal's 50-word-per-question ceiling.

## Human fields required before sending the enquiry

- author names and order;
- affiliations;
- corresponding author;
- active email address;
- ORCID for corresponding author and any coauthors who wish to supply one;
- all-author approval of the enquiry text and proposed manuscript.

Do not infer these fields from repository history.

## Presubmission email gate

Before sending:

- confirm the proposed title exactly matches `manuscript/SCH_NPH_VIEWPOINT_V1.md`;
- confirm the abstract in the enquiry exactly matches the current Summary/scientific framing;
- confirm all three question answers remain <=50 words;
- attach or paste only the material requested by the journal;
- retain the sent email and any editor response as external submission records.

## If the editor encourages full submission

The current initial-submission guidance requires:

- 1.5-line spacing;
- continuous line numbering and page numbers;
- title, full author list and institutional affiliations;
- corresponding-author email and ORCID;
- Summary;
- 5–8 keywords;
- competing-interest statement;
- author-contribution statement;
- data-availability statement;
- author-approved AI-tool disclosure where applicable.

The full cover letter should repeat answers to the same three questions (maximum 50 words each) and explicitly state that a presubmission enquiry was made.

Potential reviewers may be nominated, but nominees should not generally be coauthors/collaborators within the previous three years and should have no other conflict of interest.

## AI transparency gate

New Phytologist requires transparent description of AI-generated content/tool use in Materials & Methods or Acknowledgements depending the level of use; ordinary spelling/grammar assistance is outside this policy. Before full submission, approve wording that accurately describes any AI-assisted coding, literature triage, reproducibility work, drafting or editing that contributed to the submitted article.

## Data availability gate

New Phytologist requires a data-availability statement. For this Viewpoint/literature-synthesis paper, the final statement must distinguish source literature from repository-hosted derived ledgers, scripts and figure/readout products, and cite the permanent archive when one is frozen.
