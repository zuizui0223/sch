
The programme is not defined by pollination versus antagonism or by attraction versus defence.

## 2. Promotable general Chapter 1 result

For one shared coordinate `z`,

```text
L_S(z)=l1(z)+l2(z)
z*=argmin_z L_S(z)
L_S*=L_S(z*)
```

may be used as the general shared-architecture definition.

If the component losses are normalized to zero at their separate optima, `L_S*` may be interpreted as the residual shared-axis conflict load and the potential loss available for recovery by a larger architecture.

### Quadratic corollary

```text
L_S(z)=w1(z-theta1)^2+w2(z-theta2)^2
z*=(w1 theta1+w2 theta2)/(w1+w2)
L_S*=[w1w2/(w1+w2)](theta1-theta2)^2
```

Promotable interpretation:

> Increasing separation between the function-specific optima increases the residual cost of forcing both functions onto one trait; changing their relative weights shifts the best shared phenotype toward the more strongly weighted function.

## 3. Meaning of BALANCE

`BALANCE` means **optimization while functions remain architecturally coupled on one axis**.

Allowed Chapter 1 outcomes include:

- interior stabilizing compromise;
- directional movement on the same axis;
- context-dependent optimum shifts;
- maintained alternatives under frequency/spatial/temporal dependence;
- population-level evolutionary redirection.

Do not rewrite all of these as one interior optimum.

## 4. Floral worked-case mapping

For the floral SCH lane,

```text
z=A
W(A)=M(A)-G(A)-C(A)
```

and a local interior balance requires

```text
M'(A*)-G'(A*)-C'(A*)=0
```

with negative local curvature of total fitness.

The first-order empirical contrasts remain:

```text
M_A = change in pollinator-mediated benefit
G_A = change in antagonist-mediated cost
S_A = M_A-G_A
```

Cue overlap is one mechanism that couples both receiver channels to the same `A` coordinate. It is not the general definition of Chapter 1.

## 5. Current empirical claims that may be promoted

The existing source-adjudicated evidence may support bounded statements that:

- at least one direct observational case shows an integrated stabilizing compromise between pollinator and seed-predator selection;
- alternative floral displays can be maintained under context/frequency dependence in bounded systems;
- antagonist context can redirect population-level or experimental floral evolution;
- bouquet partitioning, conditional emission and temporal receiver separation provide mechanisms of partial cue decoupling;
- *Ficus* is a composite near-L4 historical bridge, not direct L4.

These are case-level/hypothesis-recovery results, not prevalence estimates.

## 6. Claims that are not currently supported

The strongest historical endpoint remains `NOT_EVALUABLE`, not negative.
