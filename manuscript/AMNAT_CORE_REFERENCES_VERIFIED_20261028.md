# SCH Major Article — source-verified core bibliography (2026-10-28)

These ten records are verified against the journal publishers and/or PubMed. The first nine correspond to every distinct DOI in the canonical full manuscript before this audit; the tenth is the 2019 author correction to the *Primula* study. ## Corrections, identity and evidence boundaries

- *Trillium*: Koski (2023) is the **publisher's “How to cite” year**, even though the work appeared online in late **2022** and refers to **2020–2021 field data**. A field-data year, online-first year and issue citation year must not be conflated.
- *Primula*: Chen et al. (2019) correct Figure 1 presentation and formula/notation labels for correlational selection in Table 3. It **does not change the Table 2 linear selection betas/SEs** used in SCH. This correction must accompany the 2017 primary citation where numerical treatment contrasts are discussed.
- *Brassica*: the 2016 digits in DOI `10.1007/s10682-016-9878-8` are **not the issue citation year**: the journal citation is *Evolutionary Ecology* **31** (2017), 285–304.
- *Pedicularis*: 2013, 2015 and 2016 are **three distinct original studies**, not three factorial strata from one experiment.

## Remaining scholarly citation coverage blocker

Section 7.1–7.5 of the canonical manuscript reports an adjudicated 16-cluster literature universe and names supporting cases (*Dalechampia*, *Silene*, *Satyrium*, *Fragaria*, flower-orientation negative controls, external herbivory meta-analyses), many of which lack explicit DOI-bearing citations in the full manuscript. This ten-item list verifies the already directly cited experimental backbone **only**. The additional named-source bibliographies, original evidence mapping, and complete reference matching must be finished before claiming Major Article submission readiness.
