## Role boundary — Anemone

Anemone coronaria combines colour-morph variation, natural herbivory/florivory, maternal fitness and experimental pollinator response to simulated florivory.

Glaphyrid beetles both pollinate red flowers and scratch petals. The same consumer therefore carries benefit and damage, so the record is a BENEFIT_COST_COUPLED role boundary rather than a fixed antagonist H1 conflict.

## Geographic mosaic — leafflower

The leafflower study compares floral morphology with pollinating, cheating and seed-predatory Epicephala across 16 populations.

It provides geographic trait-matching and consumer-assemblage evidence, but no common plant reproductive endpoint that would identify an H1 conflict geometry.

## Programme dependence — Camissoniopsis
