## Evidence-lane separation

### Sabatia 000626

Pollination rate is experimentally manipulated and both pollen-gain context and predispersal seed-predation risk are measured. However, the two channels are not expressed on one common plant reproductive endpoint and do not form a matched two-function response geometry. The record is therefore a P2 both-response/no-common-fitness case.

### Hakea 000628

The source compares 51 Hakea species and recovers covariance among pollination syndrome, floral colour, chemical defence and structural defence. Direct pollinator and herbivore response functions are not measured on a common fitness scale. The evidence belongs to EVOLUTIONARY_OUTCOME rather than H1 geometry.

### Silene–Hadena 000721

The focal experiment measures temporal floral VOC changes after Hadena floral interaction with and without oviposition. Although Hadena is a nursery pollinator in the biological system, the focal source does not measure pollination service or plant reproductive fitness. It therefore enters the antagonist-side design stratum rather than a two-function geometry.

### Scent synthesis 000733

The paper combines a literature synthesis with comparative floral-tissue dissections. It remains useful source-chasing evidence but is excluded from the independent primary-study count.
