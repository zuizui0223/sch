# SCH targeted primary-source expansion — 2026-09-07

## Purpose

Fill underrepresented reality-pattern classes under the sequence

```text
theory -> observable signatures -> source-adjudicated synthesis -> focal identification experiment last
```

This is a targeted expansion, not a completed systematic review and not a prevalence estimate. Under the current SCH identification paper, every case remains below the causal/pure-function promotion gate unless the source directly supports that level.

## High-confidence anchors

### Silene stellata — opposite signs through male and female function

Primary source: Zhou et al. 2020, *Evolution*, DOI `10.1111/evo.13965`.

The study reports opposite-sign sex-specific selection on petal dimensions. The authors interpret the conflict as positive male-function selection associated with pollen export by adult *Hadena ectypa* versus negative female-function selection associated with oviposition and subsequent fruit predation.

Registered class: `OPPOSING_DIRECTION`.

Claim ceiling: strong conflicting-selection evidence, but not separate bounded pure-function optima or direct SCH loss.

### Erysimum mediohispanicum — functional-weight/context shift

Primary source: Herrera 2003, *The American Naturalist*, DOI `10.1086/376574`.

Ungulate exclusion revealed pollinator-mediated selection on multiple floral and plant traits. With ungulates present, selection on floral traits disappeared and selection strength on plant morphology decreased.

Registered class: `CONTEXT_WEIGHT_SHIFT`.

Claim ceiling: context-dependent change in effective selection, not separated pure-function optima.

### Ipomopsis spp. and hybrids — sequential filters

Primary source: Campbell et al. 2022, *The American Naturalist*, DOI `10.1086/716740`.

Female fitness was decomposed into seed initiation during pollination and escape from seed predation, with selection on floral traits estimated across sequential life-history stages.

Registered class: `SEQUENTIAL_FILTER_COMBINATION`.

Claim ceiling: multispecies sequential filtering, not a simultaneous one-coordinate SCH optimum decomposition.

### Satyrium longicauda — manipulated gender compromise surface

Primary source: Ellis & Johnson 2010, *International Journal of Plant Sciences*, DOI `10.1086/656351`.

At Entabeni, pollen export peaked at the intermediate spur-length manipulation whereas female fitness was highest at the maximum tested spur length; total fitness was hump-shaped in one year.

Registered class: `COMBINED_INTERMEDIATE_OR_COMPROMISE`.

This is a strong match to shared-coordinate compromise geometry. However, only spur shortening was experimentally available, so the upper side of the female fitness function was not mapped and the female optimum cannot be declared fully bounded.

### Gymnadenia conopsea — factorial cancellation of agent-specific selection

Primary source: Sletvold, Moritz & Ågren 2015, *Ecology*, DOI `10.1890/14-0119.1`.

A factorial manipulation of pollination and herbivory separated the two selective agents. Pollinators selected for later flowering, whereas herbivores selected for earlier flowering with similar additive strength, producing little/no net phenology selection in the focal context.

Registered class: `CONTEXT_WEIGHT_SHIFT`.

This is a strong intervention-based functional-weight anchor. It is not coded as direct context-driven optimum movement because several interaction-weight regimes with separately bounded nonlinear optima were not experimentally traversed.
