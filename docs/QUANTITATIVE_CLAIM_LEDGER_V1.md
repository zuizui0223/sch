# SCH quantitative claim ledger V1

## Purpose

This ledger separates empirical/literature evidence structure from theoretical identification geometry, prospective model predictions, and quantities that the present Viewpoint deliberately leaves unpooled. It does not change the promotion ladder or the source-adjudicated evidence set.

## Claim classes

- `EMPIRICAL` — a quantity directly estimated within an individual biological study on its declared scale.
- `LITERATURE-AUDIT` — a source-adjudicated count or classification across screened systems; not an effect-size or prevalence estimate.
- `THEORETICAL-WITNESS` — a constructive geometric/identification condition showing what does or does not license a promotion.
- `MODEL-PREDICTION` — a quantitative or directional consequence of the proposed crossed-state framework, conditional on the declared experiment.
- `NOT-ESTIMATED` — a biologically meaningful cross-system quantity for which the present evidence lacks a common estimand suitable for pooling.

## Quantities explicitly not estimated

The current strict numerical inventory remains fail-closed because **fewer than three independent clusters** share a compatible estimand family, orientation, valid uncertainty, and covariance treatment.

```text
POOLED_CONFLICT_EFFECT = NOT_ESTIMATED
NATURAL_PREVALENCE = NOT_ESTIMATED
META_ESTIMATED_OPTIMUM_SEPARATION = NOT_ESTIMATED
META_ESTIMATED_CONFLICT_BUDGET = NOT_ESTIMATED
PURE_FUNCTION_OPTIMUM_DISTRIBUTION = NOT_ESTIMATED
```

The Viewpoint therefore quantifies **evidence structure and identifiability**, not a universal mean trade-off. Directional selection coefficients, treatment contrasts, bounded optimum separations, and curve parameters are not pooled merely because they are verbally related to conflict.

## Promotion rule

A numeric estimate is promotable only when its estimand matches the biological claim. Lower-rung estimates remain valid when higher-rung promotion fails: opposing local effects may be identified even when nonlinear optima are unbounded; contextual compromise may be identified even when pure-function context stability fails. Uncertainty can stop promotion.
