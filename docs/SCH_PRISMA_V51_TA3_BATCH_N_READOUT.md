## Ecologically informative retained systems

The retained set strengthens the biological rather than methodological reading of SCH.

- **Erysimum capitatum** is a direct multi-agent geographic-mosaic programme: four populations across two years combine pollinator and herbivore observations/experiments, floral traits and fitness. Pollinator and herbivore assemblages and their effects vary through space and time.
- **Sidalcea hendersonii** is retained because the gynodioecy programme couples bumblebee pollination with specialist pre-dispersal weevil seed predation.
- The 2026 **Red Queen** comparative analysis links insect-herbivore richness across 1884 angiosperms to mating/sexual traits and pollinator dependence. Its repository identity is retained separately at TA stage and report dependence is deferred to full text.
- **Veratrum album subsp. oxysepalum** measures pollination success together with herbivore/seed-predator damage across 12 populations and multiple years. Its journal and repository identities are both retained at TA stage pending formal dependence adjudication.
- **Breynia** provides a nursery-pollination boundary in which the same Epicephala lineage supplies pollination while larvae consume seeds and floral scent changes after pollination.
- Two focused syntheses are retained because they directly organize the herbivory-pollination and nectar-robbing mechanisms that source-chasing/full-text adjudication needs.

These are not new H2M1 outcomes. V51 changes no prospective predictor class, no q_j, no canonical trait-axis count and no full-text evidence-lane count.
