# BITA to SCH migration receipt

## Ownership decision

The scientific history contained two targets:

1. **BITA:** the two-trait attraction-by-defence interaction, its mechanism allocation and identifiability.
2. **SCH:** the first-order shared-cue question for one attraction/display trait.

Primary ownership of the second target now belongs here. BITA retains the five `A_to_pollination` and eight `A_to_antagonism` clusters because they remain constituent routes in its two-trait decomposition. Their retention is provenance and biological recurrence evidence; it is not a claim that BITA evaluated `S_A`.

## Migrated working products

- the one-trait estimand and shared-cue mechanism;
- the four-field coverage protocol;
- the manual adjudications of all BITA route-ledger clusters containing an A-side route;
- the comparison re-screen of the 16-system BITA identification matrix;
- the deterministic builder, generated audit, summary and readout;
- the paper-facing evidence roles for Sasidharan, Theis & Adler, Page, Junker & Blüthgen, and Knauer and colleagues.

The BITA source readouts and scripts remain versioned in BITA because the canonical two-trait route ledger still depends on their source adjudications. SCH imports frozen source tables and records their hashes rather than silently forking the BITA ledger.

## Claim boundary

- A coverage pass is not a causal estimate of `M_A`, `G_A`, or cue overlap.
- The current `1/25` is not prevalence in nature or in the literature.
- The current `0/16` reflects a two-trait-selected comparison set, not the one-trait source universe.
- Sasidharan's assembled `+12.9` percentage-point contrast is not a within-study role effect.
- No effect size is manufactured where the public source lacks the focal raw table.
