# SCH source-adjudicated reversal pathways: near-zero flips, context reweighting and same-trait fitness-component opposition (V1, 2026-11-15)

## Current new result and evidence hierarchy

On the original Caruso–Maherali–Martin 2020 unduplicated experiment compilation (DOI 10.5061/dryad.sn02v6x0q; current 137 matched gradient pairs, 26 original papers), the previously observed **41 point-estimate sign crossings** do *not* represent 41 author-certified environmental changes of selection direction.

A fresh source-joined audit finds:

| Crossings among 137 matched β pairs | Count |
|---|---:|
| Point β sign crossing | **41/137** |
| At least one treatment arm has absolute β/SE below 1 | **36/41** |
| Both arms have absolute β/SE at least 1 but at least one below 1.96 | **5/41** |
| Both arms have absolute β/SE at least 1.96 and signs oppose | **0/41** |

These divisions are based on the *same* estimated β and SE, and thus a weak-arm association is partly mathematical: **this is an evidence-strength classification, not a causal ecological predictor of reversal**. Original gradient covariance remains unknown. The conditional use of normal z-ratios also does not supersede original fitted statistical models.

## Five comparatively stronger point-crossing candidates: primary-source adjudication

All five are actual source-matched trait/treatment pairs from the frozen 137-record ledger, not five independent research projects.

| Original study | Source row ID pair | Trait / context | Original direct author test about THAT trait and treatment | Certification |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Fuselier & McLetchie 2002 (*Marchantia inflexa*) | **295/301** | Male mericell split timing, 73% to 55% shade | Male **split × shade**, original phenotypic F=**1.79**, unstarred / not significant (Table 4); genotypic F=0.10, also unstarred | **No direct signed reversal certification** |
| Trunschke et al. 2017 (*Neottia ovata*) | **429/430** | Flowering start, natural vs supplemented pollen | Flowering-start × pollination **F=6.62, P=0.010** is a **pooled 12-species test**, not a *Neottia*-specific contrast; the original species×flowering×treatment P=0.265 is likewise pooled | **Species-specific original author direct test unverified** |
| Salgado-Luarte & Gianoli 2012 (*Aristotelia chilensis*) | **530/542** | Transpiration, sun, unsprayed vs sprayed | Specific herbivory × transpiration term not reported in final selected Table 2 model | **Unverified**, not proven absent |

**Result:** no one of the five has a source-verified *individual-trait-and-context* reversal of two independently nonzero opposing arm gradients. A pooled test across species cannot certify a reversal in one orchid species, nor can missing post-stepwise regression terms be interpreted as evidence of zero effects.

## Two independent original study-level tests: environmental context changes SELECTION STRENGTH WITHOUT reversing direction

**Fuselier & McLetchie, 2002**, female *Marchantia inflexa*, greenhouse two light exposures. In the *same asexual cup-output endpoint*, selection for **earlier cup initiation** remains negative on the original standardized "number of days to cup" trait:

- 73% shade: β = **−0.820 ± 0.104**
- 55% shade (brighter): β = **−0.215 ± 0.092**.
- Source original **phenotypic** cup × shade F=**13.57, P<0.001** and **genotypic** cup × shade F=**4.14, P<0.05** (Table 4). The original source genotypic estimates are less powerful but the direct shade moderation is independently reported at genotype level.

**Salgado-Luarte & Gianoli, 2012**, field experiment with *Aristotelia chilensis* juveniles in the forest shade. Original SLA was positively associated with survival in both herbivore regimes:

- exposed to local herbivores (unsprayed): converted average logistic selection gradient **+0.120 ± 0.026**;
- herbivores excluded using pesticide (sprayed): **+0.240 ± 0.031**;
- source-fitted **herbivory × SLA** interaction χ²=**16.10, original P=0.008** (Table 3), conditional on the shaded forest understory.

The SLA β is **halved in the herbivore-exposed group** relative to insecticide exclusion. This is one specific study-level original interaction on seedling survival, *not* a general proof of herbivore selection everywhere. Other original sun models did not report a comparable SLA term, so "shade-only herbivore impact" is a supported within-shade observation, **not a demonstrated three-way light×herbivore×SLA interaction**. Potential off-target insecticide effects cannot be excluded from the archive-level comparison.

Together these examples show a real, nontrivial failure of the false dichotomy that an environmental agent either preserves all selection unaltered or reverses its direction: **multiple independent ecological systems show source-fitted strength changes while signs remain aligned**.

## A NEW biologically important positive *phenotypic conflict signature*: sexual versus asexual fitness in one cohort

The 2002 *Marchantia inflexa* paper also reports two direct phenotypic selection gradients for **the SAME trait coordinate** (higher days to *first sexual expression* means later sexual reproduction) in **the SAME 31 sex-expressing female plants under 55% shade**:

| Same shared trait coordinate | Distinct fitness endpoint | Original reported β | Original nominal significance |
|---|---|---:|---|
| Age at sexual reproduction onset | Sexual fitness proxy: number of sexual structures | **−0.915** | P<0.02, Table 6 |
| Age at sexual reproduction onset | Asexual fitness proxy: number of asexual cups | **+0.496** | P<0.05, sexual-fitness Results |

Earlier sexual expression was associated with greater sexual-fitness proxy output; later expression was associated with greater asexual cup output. The arithmetic coefficient difference (asexual minus sexual gradient) is **+1.411**, but an original *paired component-gradient difference test* and its joint covariance are **not reported**. The authors also discuss a negative sexual/asexual fitness association, but the source Table 7's correlation between number of asexual cups and sexual structures is **−0.23 with no star**, so **do not call an independently significant negative outcome covariance established**.

This has higher conceptual relevance to SCH than merely seeing a context switch of one total-selection coefficient: **two distinct reproductive pathways exhibit opposite phenotypic associations on the same measured trait in the same biological cohort**. It is a *same-coordinate fitness-component conflict signature*, not a demonstration of causal opposing **pure function optima**.

**Identification ceiling**:
- The number of sexual structures at day 150 is a proxy for sexual output, not confirmed spores/offspring; the number of asexual cups at day 120 is a proxy for asexual propagules, not confirmed total descendant fitness.
- Phenotypes were NOT experimentally randomized. Environment-induced phenotype–fitness correlations and genotype covariance may still generate apparent selection.
- The 2002 authors' genetic-mean sexual-fitness analysis did not recover significant selection for sexual onset; the phenotypic opposition is **not replicated as a genetically supported direct component opposition**.
- The two endpoints belong to **one original study and 31 shared plants**, not two independent studies. Neither treatment-specific functional optimum surfaces nor a reproductive currency weighting function were recovered. **SCH loss L is NOT identified.**

## Original 2002 negative control: total-selection sign change without verified direct trait-selection change

In male *Marchantia* under two light treatments, the original area trait changes from:

| Selection quantity on same area trait | 73% shade | 55% shade | Original area × shade |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Direct gradient β | +0.640 | −0.199 | phenotypic F=**3.31**, not significant |
| Total differential s | +0.631 | −0.135 | F=**3.90**, P<0.05 |

The original paper notes this contrast may reflect **indirect selection through correlated traits**; thus a nominally detected total-selection change is not sufficient evidence for a directly shifted optimum of area. The 55% treatment negative s was itself imprecise and not independently supported, so do not assert two significant opposite selection pressures from the two s values.

## Consequence for SCH biology and publication

Three distinct evidential objects should remain separated:

1. **Point sign instability**: 41/137 compiled pairs cross zero; 36/41 have an arm with |β/SE| below one, and even the remaining five lack author-certified opposite, significant within-treatment slopes.
2. **Real context modulation without directional flip**: original direct tests in 2002 *Marchantia* and 2012 *Aristotelia* recover trait-specific treatment modulation on cup initiation and SLA respectively, while original signed gradients remain aligned. These two independent studies complement the previous Beans & Roach competition source in PR #241.
3. **Same-trait competing fitness components**: one 31-female *Marchantia* original cohort has separately reported opposite phenotypic selection gradients on sexual-onset timing across two measured reproductive proxies. This can motivate SCH's crossed genotype/trait×P×G functional-currency experiment but does NOT by itself identify antagonistic causal optima or L.

The original paper-2020 137-pair denominator, prior 10-paper sign-persistence exact null, and existing SCH New Phytologist Viewpoint publication boundary are **unchanged**. This is new **source-adjudicated quantitative synthesis** of existing published original studies, not a new experimental plant collection or a new random-effects meta estimate.
