# SCH macroecology design recode — Batch 1

## Scope

Batch 1 contains 22 source-verified primary records already adjudicated in the early V1-V3 full-text overlays.

The batch is intentionally **outcome blind**. It codes only whether each record can support H1-H4 after trait-axis decomposition. No record is classified here as conflict, alignment, compromise, or cancellation.

This already demonstrates why the macro layer needs separate biological and epistemic analyses. Fourteen of the 22 source-verified records cannot yet enter H1 even though all 22 remain informative for H4: the literature repeatedly contains relevant biology without the design needed to identify shared-coordinate conflict.

## Trait-axis implication

Five records require explicit splitting into multiple trait axes before ecological outcomes can be coded. A further record is retained for component-level H4 coding only.

This avoids collapsing studies in which different floral traits show different ecological geometry into one study-level label.

## Next step

The eight geometry-eligible records are promoted to trait-axis recoding. Outcome coding remains deferred until each trait axis is frozen.

The remaining records are not discarded. Their missing gates enter the identification-ceiling analysis.
