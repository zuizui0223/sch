# SCH -> BITA real-world architecture evidence v1

## Purpose

This ledger reclassifies the existing literature spine under the revised dissertation architecture.

The literature is not the chapter-specific causal proof. Its role is to show that the proposed compromise and differentiation mechanisms are biologically real, to identify boundary cases, and to locate the remaining identifying experiments.

## Chapter 1 — evidence that shared multifunctionality can produce compromise

### Pérez-Barrales et al. 2013 — direct integrated-compromise anchor

The same *Dalechampia* bract-size/display axis increased pollinator visitation / pollen delivery while also increasing seed-predator exposure. The combined observational fitness function tended toward stabilizing selection.

```text
architecture role: ONE_SHARED_TRAIT_TWO_FUNCTIONS
chapter role:      DIRECT_COMPROMISE_GEOMETRY_ANCHOR
claim ceiling:     observational selection surface; no randomized do(z)
```

This is the cleanest existing real-world example of the Chapter-1 picture:

```text
pollination demand ---\
                      >--- shared display z ---> intermediate net optimum
seed-predator demand-/
```

### Theis & Adler 2012 — negative flank of the shared-trait surface

Enhanced fragrance increased florivore attraction, did not produce a detected increase in pollinator attraction, and reduced seed production.

```text
architecture role: SHARED_TRAIT_CAN_OVERSHOOT_NET_OPTIMUM
chapter role:      DIRECT_NEGATIVE_FLANK / LINKED_TRADEOFF
claim ceiling:     does not locate the full interior optimum
```

This is valuable because a compromise surface requires not only a central state but also evidence that movement along the shared coordinate can become costly when one function dominates.

### Toräng et al. 2008 — alternative compromise via polymorphism

In *Primula farinosa*, pollination success and seed predation depended on floral-display morph frequency, with the sign of frequency dependence varying across years and populations.

```text
architecture role: SHARED_TRAIT_CONFLICT_WITH_FREQUENCY_DEPENDENCE
chapter role:      POLYMORPHIC_BALANCE_REALITY
claim ceiling:     context-dependent maintenance, not one universal interior optimum
```

This shows that “balance” need not always be a single intermediate phenotype.

### Ågren et al. 2013 — causal movement when functional weights change

Long-term observations plus pollinator and grazer manipulations showed opposing selection on a genetically based floral-display dimorphism. Selection differences predicted morph-frequency change, and grazer exclusion shifted morph frequencies over time.

```text
architecture role: FUNCTIONAL_WEIGHT_CHANGE -> EVOLUTIONARY_SHIFT
chapter role:      CAUSAL_EVOLUTIONARY_BALANCE_ANCHOR
claim ceiling:     existing morph-frequency evolution, not origin of new modules
```

This is especially important for the revised SCH prediction that weakening one functional demand should move the phenotype toward the state favored by the other demand.

### Ramos & Schiestl 2019 — experimental evolutionary redirection

Multigeneration *Brassica rapa* lines showed that herbivory changed the evolutionary trajectory generated by bee pollination.

```text
architecture role: MULTIFUNCTIONAL_SELECTION_REDIRECTS_TRAJECTORY
chapter role:      EXPERIMENTAL_EVOLUTION_REALITY
claim ceiling:     broad mutualist-antagonist compromise; not strict floral same-cue z
```

## Chapter 2 — evidence that functions can become more separable

### Kessler et al. 2013 — component partitioning within one bouquet

Targeted *Petunia* scent-component perturbations showed different functional roles among volatile components: some contributed to host location while others deterred florivory.

```text
architecture role: COMPONENT_FUNCTION_DIFFERENTIATION
chapter role:      DIRECT_PARTIAL_MODULARITY_MECHANISM
claim ceiling:     no complete common-fitness test showing preserved pollination + reduced antagonist loss
```

This is a direct real-world precedent for the Chapter-2 idea that a formerly integrated signal can contain functionally distinct subcomponents.

### Knauer, Bakhtiari & Schiestl 2018 — conditional gating

Beta-ocimene attracted bees and crab spiders, florivory induced emission, and inducibility differed among populations associated with the spider context.

```text
architecture role: STATE_DEPENDENT_FUNCTIONAL_GATING
chapter role:      PARTIAL_DECOUPLING / CONDITIONAL_MODULE
claim ceiling:     shared cue retained; not private-cue evolution
```

This is not trait splitting, but it shows another way to increase effective dimensionality: function can be separated by ecological state or timing rather than by permanent trait identity.

### Kessler et al. 2015 — dimensionality without sufficient functional differentiation

Floral scent and nectar could be manipulated independently in *Nicotiana attenuata*, but both axes increased pollination-related outcomes and hawkmoth oviposition.

```text
architecture role: INDEPENDENT_TRAIT_CONTROL_WITH_REMAINING_FUNCTIONAL_OVERLAP
chapter role:      BOUNDARY_CASE
claim ceiling:     biosynthetic / experimental separability != ecological modularity
```

This is a critical negative control for Chapter 2: more trait dimensions do not automatically solve the conflict if the new dimensions remain read by both functions.

### BITA route-separation rule — recurrent architecture-level support

Across the source-adjudicated BITA evidence, separated effective routes tend to permit antagonist filtering while preserving legitimate pollination; overlapped routes tend to generate pollinator interference; bypassable routes tend to make the focal defence ineffective.

```text
architecture role: EFFECTIVE_DOMAIN_SEPARATION
chapter role:      CROSS_SYSTEM_FUNCTIONAL_DIFFERENTIATION_PATTERN
claim ceiling:     recurrence pattern, not prevalence or complete-system causal allocation
```

This is the strongest current cross-system support for the revised Chapter-2 principle: functional differentiation succeeds when the two functions experience sufficiently distinct effective trait domains.

### Kessler, Gase & Baldwin 2008 — two-coordinate reproductive improvement anchor

The manipulated benzylacetone x nicotine factorial produces a robustly positive aggregate `A x D` interaction sign under the registered compatibility analysis.

```text
architecture role: TWO_COORDINATE_FUNCTIONAL_RELIEF
chapter role:      DIRECT_OUTCOME_ANCHOR_FOR_DIMENSIONAL_RELEASE
claim ceiling:     source/design interval and clean flower-specific D scope unresolved;
                   mechanism allocation unresolved;
                   not historical modularization
```

This is the closest existing outcome-level bridge from Chapter 1 compromise to Chapter 2 differentiated architecture.

## Historical bridge — what remains missing

The current literature contains phylogenetic divergence, component partitioning, conditional gating, population change and candidate private-code systems. *Ficus* remains the strongest composite historical bridge.

But the decisive historical chain remains open:

```text
ancestral shared multifunctional z
-> identified compromise
-> derived x,y functional differentiation
-> repeated transition reconstructed
-> alternative evolutionary histories tested.
```

Accordingly:

```text
CONTEMPORARY_COMPROMISE_REALITY:              RECOVERED
FUNCTIONAL_WEIGHT_SHIFT_CAUSES_CHANGE:        RECOVERED_BOUNDED
COMPONENT / STATE DECOUPLING_MECHANISMS:      RECOVERED
TWO_COORDINATE_RELIEF_ANCHOR:                 RECOVERED_BOUNDED
COMPLETE_MECHANISM_RESOLVED_DIFFERENTIATION:  NOT_YET_RECOVERED
HISTORICAL_INTEGRATION_TO_MODULARIZATION:     NOT_YET_RECOVERED
```

## Why the two chapters are now stronger together

The existing literature no longer looks like a loose list of pollination, herbivory and defence studies. It occupies successive positions on one architecture ladder:

```text
shared multifunctionality
-> opposing functional selection
-> compromise / balance
-> conditional or component decoupling
-> two-coordinate functional relief
-> [missing] complete mechanism-resolved modularization
-> [missing] historical repeated integration-to-differentiation transition.
```

The empirical programme therefore has positive real-world support on both sides of the dissertation bridge while retaining clear claim ceilings for the two decisive missing intersections.
