# SCH JBI geography preview readout v1

## Question

Does the already source-adjudicated eight-anchor spine contain enough replicated geography or receiver-regime turnover to support the first-choice **Journal of Biogeography** route before the 868-record systematic screen is complete?

This is a preview, not the final JBI Gate J5 analysis.

## Result

```text
prior source-adjudicated anchors:                 8
clear direct geographic/interactor-regime case:   1
clear no-direct-geography / synthesis cases:       7
formal systematic full-text inclusions:            0
JBI geography gate:                                UNRESOLVED / EMPIRICALLY_SPARSE_IN_ANCHOR_SPINE
```

The one clear priority case is **Knauer, Bakhtiari & Schiestl 2018** (`10.1038/s41467-018-03792-x`). The source compares plant populations where crab spiders are present versus absent and reports stronger induced beta-ocimene emission after florivore infestation in populations with crab spiders, interpreted as evidence consistent with local adaptation. That is the kind of spatially varying interaction regime that can make geography mechanistically consequential rather than decorative.

The other seven known anchors do useful mechanistic or synthesis work but do not, under their prior adjudication, supply the same object:

- single wild system or single population;
- common-field taxon comparison;
- field-transplant host-discrimination design without replicated receiver geography;
- cross-study synthesis where source-study geography would need to be recoded explicitly;
- native-field versus experimental-tent comparison, which is an experimental setting contrast rather than biogeographic turnover.

## Claim ceiling

Do **not** conclude from `1/8` that relevant biogeographic evidence is rare in the literature. The eight studies were an inherited high-information/source-adjudicated anchor set, not a systematic geography sample.

Do **not** promote Knauer 2018 to the final JBI analysis until systematic full-text coding verifies:

```text
study_region
population/site identities
spatial grain and extent
replication of spider-present vs spider-absent states
receiver/interactor assemblage coding
historical or phylogenetic interpretation
alternative abiotic/history explanations
```

The current result is only an early journal-fit diagnostic.

## Publication implication

The first-choice JBI route is **plausible but not yet empirically secured**. The anchor spine alone is too sparse to support a broad geography × cue-overlap synthesis.

Therefore the 868-record systematic expansion has a concrete publication gate:

```text
systematic screening recovers replicated geography / receiver-turnover studies
    -> test JBI geography x cue-overlap synthesis

systematic evidence remains mostly single-system / non-spatial
    -> keep shared-cue synthesis, move first submission to Ecology and Evolution
```

A map of study locations cannot rescue a failed geography gate. The required object is replicated spatial change in interaction regimes that is analytically related to cue overlap, conflict, or cue architecture.

## Next systematic coding priority

During title/abstract screening, geography does not determine inclusion. At full text, retained records should receive the frozen JBI fields. Priority should be given to studies mentioning:

- populations or sites that differ in antagonist/pollinator/interactor presence or abundance;
- island-mainland or other biogeographic contrasts;
- replicated spatial variation in selection or consumer response;
- local adaptation, geographic mosaic, or phylogeographic transition tied to the focal cue architecture.

These are prioritization signals only; the unchanged scientific evidence lanes and claim ceilings still govern inclusion.
