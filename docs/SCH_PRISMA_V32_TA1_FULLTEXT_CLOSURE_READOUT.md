## Role-boundary result

Two additional systems are explicit BENEFIT_COST_COUPLED boundaries.

### Iris bulleyana

Sawfly florivory creates calyx holes. Bombus friseanus then changes from legitimate pollination to nectar robbing. The consumer's functional sign therefore changes with florivory-induced access state.

### Primula florindae

Lower soil moisture is associated with narrower flowers, and the dominant bumblebee shifts from legitimate visitation toward nectar robbing when floral access is constrained. Again, the same consumer cannot be assigned one fixed ecological sign.

These are not fixed antagonist H1 conflict records.
