## Why this matters biologically

These failure modes distinguish several ecological situations that are often collapsed under "multifunctionality":

1. **upstream-context studies** — herbivory, pathogens or resource state alter a floral trait and then pollinator response, but the antagonist does not respond to the same floral coordinate;
2. **shared-receiver studies without common fitness** — both audiences respond to the same cue, but their consequences cannot be compared on one plant-fitness scale;
3. **multivariate systems** — several floral traits change together and cannot yet be assigned to one shared coordinate;
4. **role-switching systems** — the same visitor can behave as pollinator, robber or exploiter depending on floral architecture/context;
5. **identified shared-coordinate systems** — both functional routes are linked to a common trait axis and can proceed to ecological outcome coding.

This makes H4 a substantive result rather than a bookkeeping appendix: the literature often contains the constituent biology while lacking the architecture needed to identify conflict.

Repeated contexts remain nested observations, not independent study replication.
