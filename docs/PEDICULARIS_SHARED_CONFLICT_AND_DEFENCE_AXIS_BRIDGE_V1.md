# Pedicularis rex shared-conflict and selective defence-axis bridge v1

## Why this system matters

`Pedicularis rex` provides an unusually clean real-world bridge between the two levels of the SCH -> BITA programme.

At the Chapter-1 level, floral exsertion beyond the water-bearing cupulate bract behaves like a shared coordinate exposed to opposing functional demands:

```text
larger corolla exsertion
-> more pollen arrival / pollination benefit
-> more seed-predator attack
-> opposing selection on the same geometric coordinate.
```

At the Chapter-2 level, water retained in the cupulate bract supplies a second antagonist-reducing functional axis that has very little detectable effect on pollinator visitation while strongly affecting seed predation and final seed production.

The key sources are:

- Sun, Armbruster & Huang 2016, Annals of Botany, DOI `10.1093/aob/mcw097`
- Sun & Huang 2015, AoB PLANTS, DOI `10.1093/aobpla/plv019`

These are separate experiments and must not be narrated as one merged factorial dataset.

## Chapter-1 evidence: shared conflict on exsertion

The 2016 study measured floral phenotype and fitness components across 14 populations. The focal composite coordinate was:

```text
corolla exsertion = (flower length - cupular-bract height) / flower length.
```

Across pooled populations:

```text
corolla exsertion -> pollen arrival: positive
corolla exsertion -> seed predation: positive
seed predation -> final viable seed production: negative.
```

The authors therefore infer opposing selection:

```text
pollinators favor greater exsertion
seed predators favor reduced exsertion / greater protection by the water-bearing bract.
```

This is a strong real-world L1 functional-conflict anchor, but not a causal SCH optimum experiment because exsertion was observational rather than experimentally assigned over multiple levels.

## Geographic functional-weight variation

The antagonist channel changes strongly among populations while the pollinator-facing direction is comparatively consistent.

Registered quantitative anchors from the 2016 article:

```text
14 populations: floral traits and pollen loads
12 populations: seed production / seed predation
299 plants / 598 flowers: pollen-load sample
mean pollen load = 12.53, SD = 5.36 grains per stigma
mean ovules = 25.96, SD = 6.33
initial seed set range = 31.10% to 48.53%
seed predation range = 0.80% to 27.42%
6 / 12 populations: final seed production significantly below initial seed set.
```

Known extremes:

```text
population 11: seed predation 0.80%
population 3:  seed predation 1.36%
population 12: seed predation 18.50%
population 5:  seed predation 27.42%.
```

The final-seed model retained population-by-seed-predation interactions and higher-order population interactions, indicating geographic variation in antagonist-mediated fitness effects.

SCH interpretation:

```text
stable pollinator-facing demand
+ geographically variable antagonist weight
-> context-dependent compromise strength / location.
```

This is external support for the prediction that weakening one functional weight can move or erase a compromise.

It does not itself estimate the SCH state optima `z_P*`, `z_G*`, or `z_C*`.

## Chapter-2 evidence: a selective defence-facing axis

The 2015 experiment manipulated water retention in the cupulate bract across six populations.

Operational contrast:

```text
intact cupulate bract with retained rainwater
vs
bract drained before anthesis.
```

The treatment had essentially no detectable effect on the pollinator-facing lane:

```text
pollinator visitation treatment effect:
beta = 0.012
SE   = 0.224
chi2 = 0.003
P    = 0.958

initial seed set treatment effect:
beta = 0.001
SE   = 0.006
chi2 = 0.014
P    = 0.906.
```

In contrast, the same treatment strongly affected antagonist-mediated outcomes:

```text
final seed set treatment effect:
beta = 0.025
SE   = 0.006
chi2 = 16.710
P    < 0.0001

seed predation treatment effect across sites:
beta = -0.072
SE   = 0.007
chi2 = 92.808
P    < 0.0001.
```

The source text states that draining the bracts increased seed predation. Seed predation increased significantly after water removal in 5 of the 6 studied populations.

Site-specific treatment tests on seed predation were significant in:

```text
Baishuitai  P < 0.0001
Sanba       P < 0.0001
Deqin       P = 0.046
Daxueshan   P < 0.0001
Shama       P = 0.005
```

and unresolved at Zhongdian (`P = 0.789`).

## Architectural interpretation

The combined evidence suggests:

```text
x-like shared / conflicted coordinate
    = corolla exsertion / exposure geometry

y-like antagonist-reducing coordinate
    = water-retention state of the cupulate bract.
```

The y-like axis shows strong preferential loading:

```text
y -> antagonist protection: strong
y -> pollinator visitation: approximately null in the experiment.
```

However, this is not complete modularization. Exsertion itself is defined partly by bract height, while the defence state is also generated by the bract. The two functions therefore occupy distinct effective mechanisms within a partly shared structure.

Best label:

```text
PARTIAL_FUNCTIONAL_DIFFERENTIATION_REALITY_ANCHOR
```

not:

```text
HISTORICAL_MODULARIZATION_PROVEN.
```

## Why it strengthens SCH

`Pedicularis rex` contributes three positive reality checks:

1. the same floral geometry is associated with opposing pollinator and seed-predator selection;
2. antagonist weight varies strongly among populations while the pollinator-facing direction is more stable;
3. a second defence-facing mechanism can be manipulated strongly without detectable pollinator-visit cost.

This means the programme no longer relies only on attraction-versus-antagonism examples in which both functions are difficult to separate experimentally.

## Missing decisive experiment

The studies do not contain a multi-level crossed surface of:

```text
exsertion x water-defence state x pollinator state x antagonist state.
```

Therefore they do not directly identify:

```text
z_P*
z_G*
z_C*
R_state
Delta_mod
```

in one experiment.

A direct follow-up would manipulate exsertion over multiple levels and cross it with intact versus drained / restored water protection while retaining selective pollinator and antagonist checks.
