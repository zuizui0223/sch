## Main scientific estimands

For the eight-cell experiment,

```text
d[g,p] = W[1,g,p] - W[0,g,p]

M_A(g) = d[g,1] - d[g,0]
G_A(p) = d[0,p] - d[1,p]
B_A    = d[0,0]
J_A    = d[1,1] - d[1,0] - d[0,1] + d[0,0].
```

Interpretation:

- `M_A(g)` — pollinator-mediated reproductive contribution of the attraction manipulation;
- `G_A(p)` — antagonist-mediated reproductive loss from the same manipulation;
- `B_A` — consumer-independent remainder, left unallocated without an independent assay;
- `J_A` — state dependence / non-separability of the two consumer channels.

The primary contemporary mechanism claim requires a validated same `A` coordinate plus positive pollinator-mediated contribution and positive antagonist-mediated loss on one common reproductive scale.

## Real-world mechanism evidence

### Route recurrence

Current source-adjudicated evidence shows that floral attraction/display traits can affect both pollination and antagonism in real systems. Same-code or same-display receiver responses occur for scent, color, morphology and display coordinates.

This supports the ecological plausibility of the SCH channel structure:

```text
A -> pollinator-mediated benefit
A -> antagonist-mediated loss.
```

### Strict linked measurement architecture

Two current studies satisfy the four-field linked gate:

```text
A manipulated
+ pollinator response measured
+ antagonist response measured
+ common plant reproductive outcome.
```

- **Theis & Adler 2012:** enhanced fragrance increased florivore attraction and reduced seed production, without a detected pollinator-attraction increase.
- **Sanchez-Lafuente 2007, Linaria lilacina:** corolla manipulation changed pollinator response and reproduction while fruit-predator visitation showed no treatment response.

Thus the measurement architecture replicates, but neither study demonstrates the central positive paired chain in which the same manipulated `A` produces both a positive pollinator-mediated contribution and a positive antagonist-mediated loss.

This is a **design-gap result** that motivates the 8-cell experiment.

### Informative near-passes

Examples retained without promotion include:

- Theis 2006: synthetic floral compounds attract both pollinators and floral herbivores on the same chemical coordinate; no common plant reproductive outcome.
- Junker & Bluethgen 2010: the same synthetic scent coordinate attracts bumblebees while repelling ants; no common plant reproductive outcome.
- Kessler et al. 2015: manipulated scent coordinate with pollinator-mediated reproduction and antagonist oviposition distributed across assay structures.
- observational geographic and selection systems: both receiver roles and reproduction are linked to floral axes, but `A` is not randomized.

Near-passes are evidence that the proposed mechanism components exist in nature. They are not failed strict experiments and must not be promoted by cross-study assembly into one synthetic factorial.

## Evolutionary-outcome grounding

The primary-source outcome audit supports bounded positive conclusions:

- integrated compromise on a shared display axis;
- context-dependent polymorphism maintenance;
- population-level evolutionary change or redirection;
- partial cue decoupling through component partitioning, conditional emission or temporal separation.

These cases establish that dual-audience conflict can have evolutionary consequences. They do not all identify SCH's contemporary channels on a common scale.

The current historical ceiling remains:

```text
private-cue evolution from a shared cue: 0 direct sources
lineage branching/specialization:        0 direct sources.
```

## Ficus historical extension

The Ficus programme remains the strongest bounded bridge toward a later historical shared/private test.

Current fixed evidence includes:

- resolved pollinator code in *F. semicordata* plus direct temporal separation of non-pollinating wasp oviposition windows;
- a ratio-specific four-VOC pollinator code in *F. carica* plus a documented non-pollinating wasp;
- pollinator and non-pollinator response to receptive odor in *F. hispida* without an equally resolved minimal synthetic pollinator code;
- a leaky/shared chemical-filter comparator in *F. auriculata*.

The key matched intersection remains empty:

```text
resolved pollinator attractive chemical codes:              2
private-code host with direct NPFW temporal separation:      1
resolved code + direct same-code NPFW behavioural response:  0
DIRECT_L4 transitions:                                      0.
```

Classification remains:

```text
COMPOSITE_NEAR_L4
not DIRECT_L4.
```

Ficus is therefore a historical extension and future same-code experiment, not the main contemporary SCH proof.

## Geography / synthesis side branch

The existing geography coding remains scientifically useful. Current full-text-coded evidence includes 22 records with both a geographic contrast and a receiver/interactor contrast in the same record.

That result makes a geographic synthesis empirically plausible, but the route remains secondary because:

- 463 frozen-cohort records remain title/abstract unscreened;
- 29 current full-text decisions remain unresolved;
- independence clustering across research programmes is incomplete;
- no common geography-by-cue-overlap estimand has yet passed scale and independence checks.

A map of study locations does not substitute for a valid geographic estimand.

## Immediate development priorities

### Priority 1 — empirical system and manipulation contract

Choose one focal biological system in which:

- one `A` coordinate can be manipulated cleanly and repeatedly;
- pollinator and antagonist access can be selectively crossed;
- the same reproductive outcome can be measured in all cells;
- manipulation checks can establish that the `A` coordinate is stable across consumer treatments.

### Priority 2 — Stage-1 pilot

Estimate:

```text
d[g,p]
M_A(g)
G_A(p)
B_A
J_A
```

plus plant/block/day/population variance and intervention leakage. Use these mechanism-scale estimates for confirmatory power planning.

### Priority 3 — confirmatory 8-cell experiment

Test the predeclared functional conflict and realized-constraint contrasts with uncertainty appropriate to the intended claims.

### Priority 4 — independent remainder assay

Do not call `B_A` a direct physiological/construction cost until an independent assay supports that allocation.

### Priority 5 — evolutionary extension

Add multiple `A` levels, repeated populations or experimental evolution only after the contemporary channel contrast is identifiable. This connects mechanism to fitness-surface shape without making historical transition a prerequisite.
