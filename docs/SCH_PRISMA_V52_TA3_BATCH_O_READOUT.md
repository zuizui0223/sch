## Biological pattern exposed by V52

V52 is unusually informative because several retained systems break the simple picture of one stable mutualist opposed by one stable antagonist.

1. **Role switching within a consumer.** In Bitou Bush, an introduced seed-herbivore biocontrol fly is also a major pollinator. In Trollius, Chiastocheta adults pollinate while their larvae consume seeds.
2. **Functional partition inside the same organ.** The nectar-plasticity study places nuptial and extranuptial rewards in the same flower but routes them to different visitor guilds.
3. **Cross-stage propagation.** Brassica leaf herbivory changes later floral morphology and volatile signals, decreases Bombus attraction and increases parasitoid attraction, with some effects persisting across generations.
4. **Shared-display dependence on context.** Tabebuia floral display is read by both pollinators and nectar robbers and is analysed across landscape disturbance.
5. **Conflict can be buffered rather than expressed as lost fitness.** Rivea combines strong pollinator dependence with florivore defence and tolerates non-vital florivory without a detectable reproductive-success penalty.
6. **Direct reproductive cost can be large.** Handroanthus nectar robbers damage many flowers and substantially reduce fruit set.

These systems strengthen a biological interpretation of SCH in which functional geometry depends on the trait coordinate, ecological context and the realized role of each consumer. They do not establish the registered prospective H2M1 class comparison.
