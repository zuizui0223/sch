# SCH Pedicularis full-surface contract v1

## Purpose

This contract is the final Chapter-1 execution layer for `Pedicularis rex` after the three preregistered preparation lanes pass in the **same population and season**:

```text
z manipulation validated
+ pollination-weight manipulation validated
+ antagonist-weight manipulation validated
-> PEDICULARIS_FULL_SURFACE_READY
-> run >=5 z x 2 P x 2 G.
```

The readiness receipt is produced by:

```text
scripts/assemble_pedicularis_full_surface_readiness.py
```

and is mandatory. A full surface must not be assembled by combining successful pilots from different populations or seasons.

## Biological mapping

The shared coordinate is:

```text
z = realized corolla exsertion above the cupulate bract.
```

The two functional-weight interventions are:

```text
P0 = SUPPLEMENTED
     flowers remain open, but saturating cross-pollen reduces dependence of
     reproductive success on natural pollinator-mediated pollen delivery

P1 = NATURAL
     open natural pollination; pollination-facing dependence remains active

G0 = PROTECTED
     cupulate-bract water retained; antagonist pressure suppressed

G1 = DRAINED
     protective water removed; antagonist pressure active.
```

Thus the registered four states are:

```text
W00 = supplemented + protected
W10 = natural      + protected
W01 = supplemented + drained
W11 = natural      + drained.
```

`P0` is **not** pollinator absence. It is a functional-weight neutralization treatment. All focal flowers remain exposed to the antagonist pathway, avoiding the confounding caused by pollinator-exclusion bags that would also alter seed-predator access.

## Primary common fitness outcome

The default system-specific wrapper uses:

```text
fitness_value = undamaged mature seed count per focal flower.
```

This is the common reproductive outcome in every cell. Do not substitute initial seed set, visitor number, or predation percentage for the primary fitness value.

Record additionally:

```text
ovule count
damaged seed count
pollen grains
early predator attack
water depth
mechanical damage.
```

These are mechanism and manipulation-check outcomes.

## Analysis

Run:

```bash
python scripts/analyze_pedicularis_full_surface.py \
  <pedicularis_surface.csv> \
  <pedicularis_readiness_receipt.json> \
  <frozen_config.json> \
  --output <sch_pedicularis_receipt.json>
```

The wrapper validates the system-specific contract, maps the raw treatments onto the generic SCH state coding, and calls:

```text
scripts/analyze_sch_compromise_surface.py
```

without changing the registered Chapter-1 estimands.

The returned SCH receipt therefore retains schema:

```text
SCH_CAUSAL_COMPROMISE_STATE_OPTIMA_V1
```

and estimates:

```text
z_P* = argmax W10(z)
z_G* = argmax W01(z)
z_C* = argmax W11(z)
state-optimum separation
shift after relaxing antagonist pressure
shift after relaxing pollination dependence
functional-component gradients
P x G context-dependence gradient.
```

## Interpretation of the pollination component

Because `P0` is saturating pollen rather than pollinator absence,

```text
W10(z) - W00(z)
```

is the reproductive consequence of remaining dependent on natural pollen delivery relative to a saturated-pollen baseline.

Its **slope / shape across z** is the relevant pollination-facing selection signal. The absolute contrast can be negative because supplementation may raise reproduction. That does not invalidate the directional component interpretation.

## Secondary system outcomes

The wrapper reports by P/G state:

```text
initial seed set
final seed set
seed-predation fraction
pollen grains
early predator attack rate
water depth
mechanical damage rate.
```

These do not replace the primary fitness analysis. They help diagnose whether the observed optimum shifts are generated by the intended functional pathways.

## Positive Chapter-1 result

The generic SCH decision remains unchanged. A positive receipt requires:

```text
distinct state optima
+ interior combined optimum
+ opposing causal optimum shifts
+ opposing functional-component gradients.
```

The result is contemporary state-specific causal compromise.

It does **not** automatically identify pure function optima.

## Optional pure-function promotion

After a positive Pedicularis receipt, the same crossed data may be passed to:

```text
scripts/identify_sch_pure_function_optima.py
```

Only context-stable component optima may be promoted to:

```text
identified_pure_function_optima.z_F1
identified_pure_function_optima.z_F2.
```

Context-dependent component optima remain conditional and are not relabelled as pure function optima.

## BITA handoff

The default BITA reference remains:

```text
z_P*
```

with dimensional release:

```text
R_state = |x0* - z_P*| - |x1* - z_P*|.
```

For the Pedicularis Chapter-2 implementation:

```text
x = exsertion coordinate
y = water-retention defence coordinate.
```

A stronger `R_pure` lane is available only if the optional pure-function upgrade passes.

## Stop rules

Stop and do not run the full surface if:

```text
readiness receipt is not PEDICULARIS_FULL_SURFACE_READY;
raw data do not match readiness population + season;
P or G labels are not the registered treatments;
<5 informative z levels remain after manipulation checks;
seed counts are inconsistent with ovule counts;
primary outcome differs across cells.
```

## Claim ceiling

A positive full-surface receipt supports:

```text
same-species contemporary causal compromise on exsertion.
```

It does not by itself establish:

```text
historical origin of exsertion-water modularity
ancestral one-trait -> two-trait transition
genetic/developmental modularity
architecture-level Delta_mod.
```
