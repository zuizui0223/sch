# Pedicularis rex empirical outcome map v1

## Purpose

This map fixes the ecological interpretation **before** the focal field outcomes
exist. It keeps enabling calibration results, causal state-surface results and
optional pure-function promotion separate.

Primary W0-W5 assignment has one source of truth:

```text
empirical/architecture/PEDICULARIS_EMPIRICAL_OUTCOME_WORLDS_V1.csv
scripts/classify_pedicularis_empirical_outcome.py
```

The optional interpretation assembler does **not** reclassify the primary
world. It attaches the independent temporal-separability result and optional
pure-function promotion receipt to that frozen W0-W5 result:

```text
scripts/map_pedicularis_empirical_outcomes.py
```

## Result hierarchy

### Outcome 0 — full surface not yet run

No causal ecological headline is licensed.

Timing, barrier qualification, P0 and P1 calibration remain enabling evidence.

### Outcome 1 — causal compromise not recovered

If the registered `z x P x G` surface fails the primary compromise criteria:

> the tested population/season does not support the registered causal
> multifunctional compromise geometry.

This is a valid negative result. The secondary enemy-displacement interpretation
is not opened.

### Outcome 2 — causal compromise recovered, enemy shift not yet evaluated

The primary SCH result is positive, but the focal ecological mechanism remains
untested:

> pollinator and seed-predator demands causally reshape the reproductive surface
> over one floral coordinate, but whether seed predators displace that optimum
> away from higher-pollination states has not yet been established.

### Outcome 3 — enemy-induced optimum displacement without pollination cost

If predator removal shifts the NATURAL-pollination reproductive state optimum
upward, but higher randomized exsertion is not supported to increase pollen
receipt:

> seed predators displace the reproductive optimum, but the registered
> pollination-performance cost of that displacement is not recovered.

This is biologically different from the headline prediction and must not be
described as pollination constraint.

### Outcome 4 — enemy-induced optimum displacement with pollen cost

If:

```text
predator removal shifts the state optimum upward
AND
higher randomized exsertion increases pollen receipt in both G states,
```

then:

> seed-predator exposure shifts the reproductive optimum toward lower exsertion,
> away from trait states with greater pollen receipt.

This is the primary biology-first headline tier.

### Outcome 5 — enemy-induced optimum displacement with pollen + initial-seed cost

If the previous result holds and higher randomized exsertion also increases
initial seed set in both G states:

> seed-predator exposure shifts the reproductive optimum away from trait states
> with both greater pollen receipt and greater pre-predation reproductive
> performance.

This is the strongest registered empirical tier.

## W0-W5 is the primary biological decision

The predeclared world classifier is authoritative for the primary empirical
paper. The assembler may not rename, collapse or rescue a W0-W5 assignment.

## Temporal separability is orthogonal

The event-time result is retained as:

- median temporal separation;
- median temporal entanglement; or
- unresolved ordering.

It determines whether the late-barrier route is biologically plausible and
describes how mutualist and antagonist windows overlap.

It does **not** upgrade or downgrade the final ecological headline once a valid
independent G and full surface have been executed. It is an enabling ecological
mechanism, not the primary response variable of the paper.

## Pure-function promotion is separate

A positive component-optimum upgrade may identify context-stable pollinator and
antagonist component optima.

Even then, the state-specific `z_P*` is not retrospectively renamed as the pure
pollinator optimum. The component optimum is a separately identified estimand.

## Claims that remain prohibited

None of the registered outcome tiers alone licenses:

```text
historical adaptation
genetic evolutionary response
predator cue identity
adaptive pollen limitation
antagonists maintain population-level pollen limitation
Delta_T50 as a confirmatory barrier hour.
```

The last two require additional causal/mediation or prospective timing evidence,
respectively.

## Paper logic

The biology-first paper can therefore remain one question across every possible
outcome:

> Does seed-predator pressure displace the reproductive optimum of floral
> exsertion away from phenotypes that perform better for pollination?

The answer can be:

```text
NO COMPROMISE
COMPROMISE BUT NO DIRECTIONAL ENEMY SHIFT
ENEMY SHIFT WITHOUT POLLINATION COST
ENEMY SHIFT WITH POLLEN COST
ENEMY SHIFT WITH POLLEN + INITIAL-SEED COST.
```

Every branch is interpretable. None requires changing the question after seeing
the data.
