# SCH literature-pattern synthesis manuscript text v1

## Existing evidence recovers recurrent conflict signatures, but not the full SCH estimand

We translated the SCH identification ladder into source-adjudicated literature signatures before prioritizing any focal causal experiment. The current ledger contains 16 independent biological clusters. Five clusters provide direct conflict signatures under the bounded literature classification: four show opposing directional selection on a shared coordinate and one shows a manipulated combined-intermediate/compromise surface. Seven additional clusters show context-dependent shifts in functional weighting or effective selection, two are aligned multifunctionality controls in which the available evidence does not support conflict, one is a shared-tracking conflict candidate with incomplete antagonist-loss attribution, and one is a sequential-filter boundary rather than a simultaneous shared-coordinate conflict estimand.

These counts describe recurrence inside a targeted, source-adjudicated evidence universe. They are not estimates of the natural prevalence of SCH conflict. Multiple papers, populations, traits, years, or outcomes from one biological programme do not create additional independent clusters.

The positive and negative classes are jointly informative. *Dalechampia*, *Castilleja*, *Pedicularis*, and *Silene* provide recurring evidence that two functional routes can impose opposing selection on the same or closely bounded trait coordinate. In contrast, flower-orientation systems such as *Abelia* and *Platycodon* show why multifunctionality alone is insufficient: the same structure can contribute to multiple functions without the available evidence establishing opposing preferred states. The literature therefore recovers both sides of SCH's first promotion gate—multifunctionality with conflict and multifunctionality without identified conflict.

## Context dependence is common, but context shift is not pure-function optimum identification

Seven independent clusters show that interaction regime, resource environment, consumer community, or antagonist pressure changes the effective selection acting on a multifunctional trait. These systems include drought-dependent floral investment, herbivore-dependent pollinator-mediated selection, multigeneration evolutionary redirection, and factorial pollinator–herbivore weighting. Such cases establish that the effective compromise geometry can be context-sensitive.

They do not by themselves identify pure function-specific optima. A change in a directional gradient is not equivalent to observing a bounded optimum move, and an optimum recovered under one ecological state is not automatically the optimum of a pure functional objective. This distinction is retained explicitly in the literature ledger and is one reason the focal component-optimum promotion gate remains necessary.

## A combined-compromise surface is recovered, but complete separated optima remain rare

The strongest current literature match to the combined SCH geometry is the manipulated spur-length study in *Satyrium longicauda*. Male pollen export peaked at an intermediate manipulated spur length, female fitness was highest at the maximum tested length, and total fitness was hump-shaped in one year. This is compatible with a combined compromise surface on one coordinate. However, the upper side of the female function was not experimentally bounded, so the study does not identify two fully bounded pure function-specific optima.

Across the current source-adjudicated universe, the most important missing literature class remains a same-system design that directly and independently identifies both pure functional optima, their separation, and the combined optimum with propagated uncertainty. The absence of that class is an identification result rather than a licence to relabel state-specific or marginal effects as pure-function optima.

## Quantitative recovery is active but pooling remains fail-closed

The quantitative inventory contains four strong same-coordinate conflict designs at different levels of numerical completeness: *Dalechampia*, *Silene*, *Fragaria*, and *Gymnadenia*. Exact component coefficients are currently available for *Dalechampia* and *Silene*. For the other designs, the relevant supplementary or treatment-level statistics require further reconstruction before a valid common contrast and its uncertainty can be frozen.

No strict random-effects stratum is currently promoted. The gate requires at least three independent biological clusters with the same estimand family, compatible orientation, valid uncertainty, and correct handling of within-study covariance. Missing covariance is not replaced by a default independence assumption, and inaccessible supplementary values are not reconstructed by eye from figures.

A second statistical boundary is equally important. If a future quantitative lane admits systems because they already show the focal opposing-sign pattern, a pooled estimate among those admitted positives is conditional on that selection. It may summarize effect magnitude and heterogeneity within positive cases, but it cannot be interpreted as an unbiased mean across all design-eligible systems or as an independent test of recurrence. Design-matched aligned/no-conflict systems must remain visible when estimating specificity or any future design-wide average.

## Literature synthesis defines the remaining causal target

The literature now supports four bounded conclusions. First, same-coordinate opposing functional signatures recur across independent biological systems. Second, comparable multifunctional systems can lack identified conflict. Third, ecological context can strongly change effective functional weighting. Fourth, combined-compromise geometry can be recovered in at least one manipulated system, but complete pure-function optimum identification remains rare.

What the literature does not identify is equally clear. It does not jointly recover the four reproductive state surfaces `W00(z)`, `W10(z)`, `W01(z)`, and `W11(z)` for a qualified focal system, test the predicted optimum shifts under selective consumer removal, establish opposing functional-component gradients near the combined optimum, or pass the context-stability gate required to promote state-specific component optima to pure-function optima. It therefore does not yet justify a direct empirical SCH conflict budget `L` for downstream transport.

The focal `z x P x G` experiment belongs after this synthesis, not before it. Its role is no longer to demonstrate that the constituent biology exists; the literature already establishes that. Its role is to close the specific identification gap that existing studies leave open: causal state-surface reconstruction and, where the data allow, promotion from contextual component optima to context-stable function-specific optima.

## Manuscript transition

The empirical ordering for the active SCH paper should therefore be:

```text
theory and identification ladder
-> source-adjudicated recurrence / specificity map
-> compatible quantitative inventory
-> explicit literature identification gap
-> final focal causal validation programme
```

This ordering preserves SCH's narrow claim ceiling while allowing the empirical half of the paper to stand on cross-system reality-pattern recovery rather than on an unexecuted focal experiment.
