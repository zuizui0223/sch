# SCH quantitative meta-analysis promotion rule v1

## Purpose

The primary empirical layer is reality-pattern recovery. Quantitative pooling is available only for compatible sub-lanes; it is not required to establish that SCH-linked signatures recur across independent systems.

A critical distinction is frozen here: if entry into a quantitative lane depends on observing the focal opposing-sign pattern, any pooled mean computed only among those admitted positives is **conditional on positive admission**. It is not an unbiased mean across all design-eligible systems and cannot serve as an independent statistical test of recurrence.

## Unit of independence

The independent unit is the biological study cluster. Multiple functions, years, populations, doses, treatments, traits, or outcomes from one underlying panel do not create additional independent clusters.

## Design-eligible versus positive-admitted systems

For any future quantitative synthesis maintain two nested sets:

```text
design-eligible systems
-> meet trait, function, estimand, scale, and uncertainty requirements regardless of sign

positive-admitted systems
-> design-eligible AND show the preregistered opposing-component pattern
```

Design-matched aligned/no-conflict systems must remain visible as specificity controls. They cannot be discarded and then treated as if the positive-only pool represented the full eligible evidence universe.

## Strict component-conflict lane

A system may enter the strict numerical inventory only when it contains, within the same biological cluster:

1. the same shared coordinate or trait;
2. at least two function-specific or agent-mediated selection components;
3. a common trait standardization and interpretable fitness scale within the study;
4. source-verified numerical estimates;
5. identified uncertainty sufficient for the intended contrast.

Opposing component directions define a positive conflict signature, not the basic design-eligibility gate for a future design-wide synthesis.

Dalechampia, Silene, Fragaria, and Gymnadenia currently supply strong same-coordinate conflict designs at different levels of numerical completeness. The current ledger also preserves aligned/no-conflict systems outside the positive numerator.

## Random-effects promotion gate

Do not calculate a pooled effect merely because at least three studies exist. A quantitative stratum is promoted only when all of the following hold:

```text
independent biological clusters >= 3
same estimand family
compatible effect orientation
valid uncertainty for each effect
within-study covariance handled when an effect is a difference of component estimates
no duplicate biological panel counted twice
```

If a source reports component estimates but not their covariance, do not assume independence by default. If the exact supplementary coefficient or uncertainty is inaccessible, retain the row as `NUMERIC_EXTRACTION_PENDING` rather than reconstructing a convenient value from a figure.

## Interpretation of any future pool

Two different pools have different meanings:

```text
design-wide pool
= includes all compatible design-eligible systems under a sign-independent inclusion rule
= may estimate a design-universe average if the evidence universe itself supports that interpretation

positive-admitted pool
= includes only systems already selected for the focal opposition pattern
= conditional magnitude / heterogeneity summary only
= NOT an independent recurrence test or unbiased general mean
```

Recurrence of the qualitative SCH conflict signature is supported by independent recovery of the preregistered pattern across biological clusters, with aligned/no-conflict systems retained to establish specificity.

## What may be synthesized before pooling

Before the random-effects gate is met, report:

```text
number of independent strict conflict designs
number with exact component coefficients
number with uncertainty-bearing estimates
sign/opposition recurrence
aligned/no-conflict controls
context moderators
complete-optimum identification gaps
```

This is a systematic quantitative inventory, not a pooled general effect.

## Claim ceiling

Even a valid standardized selection synthesis would not equal theoretical conflict loss `L`, and it would not identify pure function-specific optima unless the relevant component surfaces and promotion assumptions were directly supported. State-specific reproductive optima remain distinct from pure-function optima by default.

## Focal experiment position

The selective multi-level `z x P x G` causal surface remains the final identification upgrade after cross-system pattern recovery. It is not the entry point to SCH's empirical justification.
