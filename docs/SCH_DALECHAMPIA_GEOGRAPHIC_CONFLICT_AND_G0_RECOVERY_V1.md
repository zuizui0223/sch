# SCH Dalechampia geographic-conflict and G0 recovery v1

## Decision

`Dalechampia scandens` remains the strongest case-level reality anchor for Chapter-1 compromise, but it is **not a universally conflict-active species**.

Two primary studies now define the execution boundary.

### Mexico 2006 population — positive conflict anchor

Pérez-Barrales et al. (2013; DOI `10.1111/j.1600-0706.2013.20780.x`) studied one population near San Juan de Villa Rica, Veracruz, Mexico. Larger bracts received more bee visits and pollen, and blossoms with larger bracts had higher seed-predation probability. Their integrated female-fitness surface tended toward stabilizing selection on bract size.

Important claim boundary: the paper interpreted the predation pattern as adult curculionid oviposition choice **conditional on the assumption** that the pattern was not instead generated by differential larval success or inter-inflorescence larval movement. Thus the natural conflict is a strong phenotypic-selection anchor, but direct observation of the exact adult oviposition window is not recovered.

### Costa Rica 20-population study — conflict is geographically contingent

Opedal et al. (2019; DOI `10.1002/ajb2.1209`) tested three focal populations repeatedly and a broader set of 20 populations in northern Costa Rica. Seed-predation probability was largely unrelated to floral advertisement within focal populations and among populations, and seed-predator variation had only minor modifying effects on pollinator-driven advertisement evolution.

Therefore:

```text
Dalechampia multifunctionality:             RECOVERED
Dalechampia case-level bract conflict:       RECOVERED
Dalechampia species-wide conflict:           NOT SUPPORTED
geographic turnover in antagonist effect:    RECOVERED
```

The positive Chapter-1 experiment must not select a population merely because it is `D. scandens`.

## Consequence for system selection

Dalechampia is now a **conditional first-choice system**:

```text
first screen a candidate population
    ↓
confirm sufficient seed-predator incidence
    ↓
confirm bract-display association with both pollination and predation
    ↓
only then promote that population to the causal compromise experiment.
```

Failure to find a conflict-active population is a biological result, not a failed manipulation. In that case Dalechampia remains a real-world case anchor and SCH should move the causal experiment to another system.

## Stage 0P — population qualification screen

Before building the 20-cell experiment, screen candidate populations / seasons using natural blossoms.

Minimum readouts:

```text
measured apparent bract area
pollinator visitation / pollen receipt
seed-predator incidence and damaged-seed count
seeds initiated / mature intact seeds
plant, patch, date, phenological phase
```

Promotion requires all of the following:

```text
P0.1 seed-predator incidence is high enough for an experimental G contrast
P0.2 pollination increases with the declared bract-display coordinate over the candidate range
P0.3 predation also responds to that coordinate or to a tightly linked same-blossom state
P0.4 the two functional responses imply non-aligned preferred states over an experimentally reachable range
```

No fixed numerical threshold is declared before a pilot variance / incidence distribution is available. The confirmatory threshold must be set prospectively from pilot data.

## What is known about blossom timing

The Mexican study reports receptive blossoms lasting up to about 10–12 days: roughly 1–3 days of female phase followed by a 7–10 day bisexual phase. Bracts open and close daily while receptive and remain closed during fruit maturation.

This creates a natural opportunity for sequential exposure, but **the Nanobaris oviposition window itself has not been recovered to a precision that licenses a timing-based G0 treatment**.

Therefore broad statements such as

```text
pollination happens first
seed-weevil oviposition happens later
```

are not currently allowed for the focal system.

## Stage 0G — recover a selective seed-predator intervention

The first-choice pilot is not insecticide and not permanent bagging. It is **controlled sequential exposure**.

### G-pilot design

Use naturally or hand-pollinated blossoms with the same validated `z` manipulation and assign them to short, predeclared adult-weevil exposure windows:

```text
E0 no adult-weevil exposure
E1 female-phase exposure
E2 early bisexual exposure
E3 late bisexual exposure
E4 post-receptive / early fruit-development exposure
```

Exact windows are updated from local phenology before the experiment; the categories above are the identification logic, not frozen calendar days.

For exposure treatments:

```text
standardize adult-weevil number / sex when identifiable
standardize exposure duration
use a mesh / chamber that leaves the bract display and pollinator treatment unchanged outside the exposure window
record adult contact / feeding / probing when observable
track the blossom to seed development
score predation / larval emergence / damaged seeds
```

This pilot asks two questions simultaneously:

1. **When can adult exposure create later seed predation?**
2. **Can exposure be switched on and off without changing pollination, the bract coordinate, resin reward, or post-pollination bract function?**

### Why controlled introduction is preferable to passive exclusion

The current literature does not supply a validated fine-scale exclusion method specific to Nanobaris. Controlled introduction provides a direct positive manipulation of the antagonist route and allows a zero-exposure reference without assuming that natural access is temporally separable from pollination.

The design is promoted only if introduced adults generate a reproducible increase in later seed predation and the chamber / handling sham has negligible effects on pollen receipt, fertilization and seed development.

## Direct oviposition measurement

If eggs or oviposition scars can be visualized non-destructively, add them as mediator measurements. If they cannot, do not label later seed damage as directly observed oviposition. Use the bounded chain:

```text
controlled adult-weevil exposure
    -> later predation / larval damage
```

and retain adult oviposition choice as an inferred intermediate until independently measured.

## G0/G1 promotion rule

A selective antagonist intervention is admitted only if:

```text
G1 controlled weevil exposure materially increases later seed loss
G0 no-exposure / exclusion keeps later seed loss low
pollinator visitation / pollen receipt is equivalent across G states conditional on P treatment
measured z, resin reward, bract posture and post-pollination bract integrity are preserved
```

If these gates fail, Dalechampia cannot support the mechanism-resolved L3 claim even if natural opposing selection is real.

## Scientific value of a failed population screen

A failure to recover conflict in a new population would support a broader version of SCH rather than invalidate it: the balance depends on the weights of the functions. Geographic turnover in `w2` should move or erase the compromise optimum. The 2013 versus 2019 contrast is therefore itself consistent with the framework prediction that compromise is context-dependent when the antagonist function is weak or absent.
